    Categories

    Rename, recolor or reorder. Deleting a category keeps its tasks; they just lose the label. Tip: type #name while adding a task to use or create a category.

      Background

      Use your own picture. It is saved in your account and shows on every device where you sign in. PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF; animated GIFs up to 3 MB stay animated, bigger photos are made smaller.

      Your data

      Import accepts files from this app and from the old Task Manager.html (Download backup). You can also drop the file anywhere on the page. Tasks you already have are skipped.

      Typing shortcuts in the add box

      today tomorrow fri next mon 20.11 nov 20 in 3 daysDo on that day
      due fri by 20.11 deadline next weekSet a deadline
      daily every weekday every mon and thu every 2 weeks monthlyRepeat (completing moves it to the next date)
      #workCategory (new name creates one)
      Paste several linesAdds one task per line
